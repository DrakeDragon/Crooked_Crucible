# ⚒️ The Crooked Crucible

**A crafting LitRPG that lives in your phone's browser.**

You inherit a ruined forge, a sarcastic anvil, and ten crafts to master. Gather, smelt, weave, brew, enchant and cook your way up through eleven tiers of gear, serve a town of very particular customers, chase eight legendary relics, then take what you've made into battle against hundreds of monsters. Funny, deep and built to be hard to put down.

---

## Features

### Crafting (the heart of the game)
- **10 crafts** — smithing, armoring, woodworking, weaving, jewelcrafting, arcane enchanting, alchemy, cooking, tools and gadgets — each with its own mentor, story, perks, titles and station upgrades (levels 1–10).
- **950 recipes** across **11 material tiers** (Copper up through the endgame Tier XI), with quality grades up to Legendary.
- Craft levels cap at 60; your hero caps at **level 80**.
- A deep gathering game: dozens of zones, tools, apprentices who gather while you're away, and an offline catch-up.
- A living market: shifting demand, a rotating merchant, and **25 recurring customers** who post commission orders.
- **8 legendary Relics** — extremely hard to obtain, forged from rare fragments.
- **265 achievements**, talents, ranks and renown.

### Combat and hunting
- **37 hunting grounds** with **185 monsters** — mobs, elites and a boss in every zone.
- **Gear gates:** a zone only opens when your total Gear Score is high enough.
- **Your gear is the fight.** Weapon style (Heavy / Swift / Arcane), elemental weapons and wards, armor, quality and talents all change the outcome. Enemies are Beasts, Armored, Ethereal, Swift, Brutes or Casters, each with weaknesses and telegraphed attacks.
- Hunt, Elite hunt, Boss and 5-wave Gauntlet modes, plus skills, guard, food, potions and gadgets.
- Every fight shows the enemy on screen. A Bestiary tracks everything you've defeated.

### Story
- A guided, multi-chapter **tutorial story** that teaches the game as it unfolds.
- Two full acts of story chapters and a funny, escalating **endgame story** ending in a final boss fight.
- 70 side tales across the crafts.

### Endgame
- Tiers **IX–XI** are a separate gear set whose materials drop only from monsters, and every piece needs a boss-dropped Core.
- Seven endgame zones where a Superior kit struggles and a Legendary kit is expected.

## Saves
Progress is stored in the browser's localStorage on each device and does not sync between devices. Use **Settings → Export** to copy a save code and **Import Save** on the title screen to restore it. On iPhone, a home-screen install can have separate storage from Safari, so export before installing.
